ZnKeyboard 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 4 日

English

ZnKeyboard 是带 AI 润色和翻译的输入法，另有 macOS 和 Windows 版，由开发者 Felix Yung 提供。本政策说明这些应用会处理哪些数据。

我们没有账号系统，也没有中转服务器

使用 ZnKeyboard 不需要注册。你的文字不会经过我们的服务器，我们也无法看到你的文字、语音或 API Key。

普通打字

平时打字的内容留在设备上，不会发送到网络。输入法会在本机学习你的用词和按键习惯，以提升输入准确度；密码框和要求不学习的输入框不会参与学习。

AI 润色、翻译和语音输入

只有在你主动使用这些功能时，才会发送数据：

  • 文字：你选择润色或翻译的那段文字，会从你的设备直接发送给你自己配置的 AI 服务商。
  • 语音：使用 AI 语音输入时，录音会直接发送给你配置的 AI 服务商处理。只有按住说话时才会录音，应用需要麦克风权限。
  • 截图翻译（Mac、Windows）：截图只在你的电脑上识别，图片不会上传，只有识别出的文字会发给服务商。

这些内容由你选择的服务商（例如 DeepSeek 或 Google Gemini）按其隐私政策处理，请自行查看。例如，Google 可能会把免费额度下的内容用于改进产品，也可能由人工查看。请不要把不愿让服务商处理的内容发送给 AI 功能。

保存在设备上的数据

  • API Key：只保存在你的设备上，并排除在 Android 系统备份之外（Android 密钥库、macOS 钥匙串、Windows 凭据管理器）。
  • 设置和学习数据：保存在设备上。Android 的系统备份如已开启，可能包含设置等应用数据，但不包含 API Key。
  • 剪贴板历史：只保存在设备上，用于键盘里的剪贴板功能，可以随时清除。

匿名使用统计

如果开启，应用每天最多发送一次匿名统计到 zn.2amz.com，用于统计用户数：随机生成的安装编号、平台、版本和使用次数。其中不含任何文字，也不包含姓名、邮箱或设备标识符，我们不存储 IP 地址。你可以在设置里随时关闭：Android 在「备份」页的「关于」，Mac 和 Windows 在「通用」。

应用权限（Android）

  • 网络：用于发送你主动发起的 AI 请求和匿名统计。
  • 麦克风：仅用于 AI 语音输入，由你按住说话时使用。
  • 开机后运行：让每日匿名统计在重启后继续排程。

共享与出售

我们不出售数据，也不用于广告。除上述你主动发送给 AI 服务商的内容外，我们不向第三方共享数据。

儿童

ZnKeyboard 不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，我们也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

删除数据

卸载应用会删除设备上的应用数据。匿名统计只有随机编号，无法对应到你本人；如需处理，可以通过下方方式联系我们。发送给 AI 服务商的数据，请向对应服务商申请处理。

政策变更

政策更新时，我们会修改本页并更新生效日期。

联系我们

如有问题，请发送邮件至 oprfelix@gmail.com，或在 GitHub 上联系我们。

ZnKeyboard Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

ZnKeyboard is a keyboard with AI polish and translation, with companion apps for macOS and Windows, provided by the developer Felix Yung. This policy explains what data the apps handle.

No accounts, no relay servers

ZnKeyboard needs no sign-up. Your text doesn't pass through our servers, and we can't see your text, voice or API key.

Everyday typing

What you normally type stays on your device and isn't sent anywhere. The keyboard learns your words and touch habits on the device to type more accurately. Password fields, and fields that ask not to be learned from, are left out.

AI polish, translation and voice input

Data is sent only when you use these features yourself:

  • Text: the text you choose to polish or translate goes directly from your device to the AI provider you set up.
  • Voice: with AI voice input, your recording goes directly to the AI provider you set up. The app records only while you hold to talk, and needs microphone permission.
  • Screenshot translation (Mac, Windows): screenshots are read on your computer and never uploaded. Only the text found in them goes to the provider.

The provider you choose, such as DeepSeek or Google Gemini, handles this content under its own privacy policy, so please review it. For example, Google may use content sent on its free tier to improve its products, and people may review it. Don't send anything to the AI features that you wouldn't want the provider to process.

Data kept on your device

  • API key: stays on your device and is left out of Android system backups (Android Keystore, macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager).
  • Settings and learned data: stay on your device. Android system backup, if you have it on, may include settings and other app data, but not the API key.
  • Clipboard history: stays on your device for the keyboard's clipboard feature, and you can clear it at any time.

Anonymous usage count

When it's on, the app sends an anonymous count to zn.2amz.com at most once a day, to count users: a randomly generated install ID, the platform, the version and the number of uses. It contains no text, name, email or device identifier, and we don't store IP addresses. You can turn it off at any time: on Android under About on the Backup page, and on Mac and Windows under General.

App permissions (Android)

  • Network: sends the AI requests you start and the anonymous count.
  • Microphone: used only for AI voice input, while you hold to talk.
  • Run after restart: keeps the daily anonymous count scheduled after a restart.

Sharing and sale

We don't sell data or use it for advertising. We don't share data with third parties, other than what you send to your AI provider yourself.

Children

ZnKeyboard isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app deletes its data from your device. The anonymous count holds only a random ID that can't be tied to you; contact us below if you have a request. For data sent to an AI provider, contact that provider.

Changes

When this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.

Contact

For questions, email oprfelix@gmail.com or reach us on GitHub.
